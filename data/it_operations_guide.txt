1. Remote Access and VPN
Employees working outside the corporate office must connect through the WireGuard Corporate VPN client before accessing internal Git repositories, staging environments, or databases. Split tunneling is disabled on production profiles.

2. Software Installation Requests
Standard developer tools on the pre-approved catalogue may be installed through the internal self-service portal. Any third-party binary or browser extension outside the catalogue requires a ticket filed with the IT Service Desk and security review approval before installation.

3. IT Helpdesk SLA
Critical incidents affecting production access or account lockouts are acknowledged within 30 minutes during business hours (09:00 to 19:00 IST, Monday to Friday). Standard hardware or access requests are fulfilled within 2 business days.
