1. Password Policy
All employees must use passwords containing at least 12 characters. Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days and cannot reuse any of the last 5 passwords. Sharing credentials with colleagues or storing plaintext passwords in shared drives is strictly prohibited.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for accessing corporate email, SSO portals, and cloud consoles. Employees must complete a second verification step using either the approved mobile authenticator app or a company-issued hardware security key. SMS-based one-time passcodes are permitted only as a temporary fallback approved by IT Security.

3. Workstation Security
Company laptops must be configured to auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity and use full-disk encryption at all times. Any lost or stolen corporate device must be reported to the Security Operations team within 2 hours of discovery so remote wipe procedures can be initiated.
